## 2. Individual Stakeholder Descriptions

### 2.1 Primary Stakeholders

#### 1. Individual Users (Students and Working Professionals)

Individual users are the primary users of the Smart Expense Splitter and Tracker. They use the application to record personal expenses, organize transactions, manage budgets, and understand their spending habits. The application helps them review their financial activities and make informed spending decisions.

**Responsibilities:**
- Record and track personal expenses.
- Categorize transactions.
- Create and monitor budgets.
- Review spending patterns and summaries.
- Manage personal expense records.

#### 2. Group Users (Friends, Roommates, and Colleagues)

Group users are people who share expenses with friends, roommates, or colleagues. They use the application to record shared costs, check their individual expense shares, and monitor pending balances. This helps improve transparency and simplifies group expense management.

**Responsibilities:**
- View shared group expenses.
- Track individual contributions.
- Check outstanding balances.
- Monitor shared spending.
- Coordinate expense settlements.

#### 3. Group Admin / Creator

The Group Admin or Creator is responsible for creating and organizing expense groups. This stakeholder manages group membership and oversees shared expense records to help ensure that group activities remain organized.

**Responsibilities:**
- Create expense groups.
- Add or remove group members.
- Manage group information.
- Oversee shared expense records.
- Monitor group balances and settlements.

### 2.2 Secondary Stakeholders

#### 4. Company Owners

Company owners are responsible for defining business objectives and monitoring the application's overall performance and sustainability. They evaluate whether the application meets user needs and supports long-term business goals.

**Responsibilities:**
- Define business objectives.
- Monitor application performance.
- Evaluate user satisfaction.
- Support scalability and sustainability.
- Identify opportunities for improvement.

#### 5. Developers / IT Teams

Developers and IT teams are responsible for developing, testing, maintaining, and improving the application. They implement features, resolve technical problems, and help ensure system reliability and security.

**Responsibilities:**
- Develop and maintain application features.
- Test application functionality.
- Fix bugs and technical issues.
- Improve system performance.
- Maintain application reliability and security.

#### 6. Customer Support Teams

Customer support teams assist users who experience difficulties while using the application. They explain application features, respond to queries, and help resolve reported problems.

**Responsibilities:**
- Respond to user questions.
- Explain application functionality.
- Assist with reported issues.
- Guide users through expense-management workflows.
- Coordinate technical problems with developers.

#### 7. GenAI Service

The GenAI Service supports intelligent expense-management features. Depending on the application's implemented functionality, it can assist with expense categorization, analyze expense descriptions, and generate summaries that help users understand their spending patterns.

**Responsibilities:**
- Assist with expense categorization.
- Analyze expense descriptions when supported.
- Generate expense summaries.
- Provide spending insights.
- Support intelligent expense analysis.

#### 8. Bill/OCR Service

The Bill/OCR Service processes uploaded bills and receipts to extract relevant expense information. It helps reduce manual data entry by identifying available information from receipt images or documents.

**Responsibilities:**
- Process uploaded bills and receipts.
- Extract text from receipt images.
- Identify relevant expense details.
- Reduce manual expense-entry effort.
- Support receipt-based expense recording.
